# Saxophone Sight Reader music credits

The library contains newly sequenced MIDI files for single-line sax practice.
Each file uses written sax pitch. The editable melodies are in
[arrangements.json](arrangements.json). The generated MIDI and inspections carry
the same GPL-3.0-only grant as the new arrangement material. The historical
sources retain their own public-domain status. The library includes no source
scans, recordings, lyrics or accompaniment.

## Historical melodies

- **Swing Low, Sweet Chariot (refrain)** is a traditional African American
  spiritual often attributed to Wallace Willis. This shortened refrain uses
  the melody from the Fisk Jubilee Singers' early setting, transcribed by
  Theodore F. Seward. The adaptation is in written G major and makes space
  to breathe at the ends of the phrases. The 1873 songbook page is identified
  as public domain. [Fisk edition, p. 166](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:SwingLowSweetChariot1873.jpg)
  · [Baylor University copy of the Fisk collection](https://digitalcollections-baylor.quartexcollections.com/Documents/Detail/jubilee-songs-as-sung-by-the-jubilee-singers-of-fisk-university-nashville-tenn.-under-the-auspices-of-the-american-missionary-association/2432698)
- **Indiana (chorus)** is by James F. Hanley, with words by Ballard MacDonald
  in the original edition. The sax line follows the 1917 Shapiro, Bernstein &
  Co. vocal chorus in G major, with breathing space and one ending. The
  Lester S. Levy Sheet Music Collection scan carries a Public Domain Mark.
  [Levy Collection](https://levysheetmusic.mse.jhu.edu/collection/152/200)
  · [1917 scan and rights notice](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:(Back_Home_Again_in)_Indiana.pdf)
- **The Entertainer (opening strain excerpt)** is by Scott Joplin. This
  single line takes the melody from the pickup into bar 5 through bar 16,
  with short breath rests. Chris Sawer typeset the Mutopia edition from the
  1902 original. Mutopia explicitly permits redistribution and modification
  of the dedicated edition and MIDI. The source MIDI's SHA-256 is recorded
  in the catalogue and the editable arrangement.
  [Mutopia edition 263](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/cgibin/piece-info.cgi?id=263)
  · [Mutopia's public-domain dedication](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/legal.html#publicdomain)

## Original saxophone tunes

**Blue Platform** (12-bar blues), **Corner Table** (swing), **Last Light**
(ballad), **Harbour Walk** (bossa nova) and **Green Signal** (funk) were
written for this project. The written swing eighths play evenly in the
MIDI preview, so you can supply your own swing feel on the sax.

See the [GPLv3 text](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/gpl-3.0.html) for the new
material. The [catalogue](catalogue.json) records the editions and hashes of
the reviewed references, new arrangements and new MIDI files.
